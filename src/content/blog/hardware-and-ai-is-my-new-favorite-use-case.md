TONS of people are writing code, creating apps and their own stuff with the help of AI. That's awesome. 

In my opinion the future where you can still be a tech expert, make money and provide the most value is in hardware and experiential things. 

My new favorite use case of AI at the moment is harvesting hardware components and creating useful tech.

Yesterday my Chromecast remote quit working. I went to Walmart and bought the new Onn media device, but wanted to see what I could harvest from the old chromecast. Apparently A LOT! I got perfect instructions to harvest multiple components and ideas of what to create with them. 

THIS is the future
